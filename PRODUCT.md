# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Egghead learners use the private profile while managing their account identity, understanding their current learning access, and reviewing recent progress. They may share a separate public profile with peers, colleagues, or prospective collaborators who want a concise view of their learning activity without exposing private account details.

## Product Purpose

The profile experience gives learners one trustworthy place to understand who they are signed in as, what learning they can access, what they have completed, and what is safe to share publicly. Success means learners can scan their account state quickly, make the one supported identity change confidently, and share a public learning record without wondering whether private data is exposed.

## Brand Personality

Warm, tactile, confident. The experience should retain Egghead's handmade paper, yolk, and navy character while behaving like a focused product workflow rather than a promotional page or novelty dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard card grids, trophy-heavy gamification, ornamental AI gradients, excessive status pills, repetitive explanatory copy, and decorative surfaces that compete with account or learning information. Do not make the public profile feel like a social network or the private profile feel like an enterprise administration console.

## Design Principles

- Make account state immediately legible and trustworthy.
- Let learning activity tell the story instead of manufacturing achievement theater.
- Separate private identity controls from the public learning projection.
- Keep Egghead character in typography, material, and small moments, not UI clutter.
- Prefer one clear next action over competing calls to action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve full keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic headings and landmarks, sufficient contrast in both color schemes, and status communication that does not rely on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep responsive reading order logical at narrow widths.

## Connected Account Safety

Connected-provider details remain private. A learner may disconnect only an exact provider account owned by their current session, and only when another sign-in account supported by the current Egghead auth configuration remains. Phase 0 supports GitHub sign-in only, so a sole GitHub identity is intentionally non-removable until another supported sign-in method exists.
